Poland's software engineering market, decoded for 2025–2026.

The Eldorado era is over. The market has matured into a bifurcated system that rewards mid/senior generalists who embrace AI tooling, squeezes juniors, and relocates value from pure coding to cloud, data, AI/ML and platform work. Here's the whole landscape — boards, contracts, taxes, salaries, negotiation — in five short chapters.
