Chapter 02 · Contracts & tax

UoP or B2B? It's arithmetic, not preference.

For mid-to-senior developers the choice is binary. Above ~15,000–18,000 PLN/month, B2B on the 12% ryczałt yields materially more take-home than UoP — at the cost of paid leave, sick pay and statutory notice.

Ryczałt still wins, because of the 50% deduction
Ryczałt payers deduct 50% of paid health contributions from revenue (not from tax). A typical 12% ryczałt programmer has an effective health-contribution cost of ~780 PLN/month rather than 831 PLN. Post-veto reversion to Polski Ład rules reinforces, not weakens, the ryczałt 12% advantage for IT.

Where juniors land
Juniors disproportionately start on UoP — typically in corporate, consultancy and SSC/GBS hubs like Sii, Capgemini, EPAM, Accenture and Luxoft. Mid and senior engineers then migrate to B2B once the tax math starts paying for itself.
