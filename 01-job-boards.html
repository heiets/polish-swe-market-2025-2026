Chapter 01 · Where to look

Where Polish engineers actually find work.

Poland has the most competitive IT job-board landscape in CEE. Four majors, four specialists, one clear rule: salary transparency shifts the market. Mid & senior developers gravitate to the boards that mandate it.

Why mandatory transparency matters
Salary transparency is mandatory on Just Join IT, NoFluffJobs and Solid.jobs. That's why mid and senior developers gravitate there — the published upper bound is a negotiation anchor. On Pracuj.pl and LinkedIn, only a minority of ads disclose pay.

How to read the landscape by seniority

The same market looks completely different depending on where you sit on the experience curve.

The #1 candidate complaint, by a distance
