Chapter 05 · Playbook

The playbook, the caveats & what to do this week.

Seller's market for mid/senior generalists — especially those fluent in cloud, data & AI tooling. Buyer's market for juniors. B2B on ryczałt 12% is the dominant optimisation above ~18,000 PLN/month. Here's the distilled action list.

Sub-section 5.1

The playbook · four load-bearing facts

If you only remember four things from this guide, make it these.

01 · Market shape
Seller's market for mid & senior. Buyer's market for juniors.
The Eldorado era of 2021–2022 is gone. The market has matured into a bifurcated system that rewards mid/senior generalists who embrace AI tooling and squeezes juniors (4.79% of offers, 370 applications per ad).
02 · Contract shape
B2B ryczałt 12% dominates above ~18,000 PLN/month.
Typically yields 20–35% more net take-home than UoP at the same employer cost. 2026 post-veto reversion to Polski Ład rules reinforces rather than weakens the ryczałt advantage for IT.
03 · Geography
Warsaw tops, Kraków closed the gap
Kraków, Wrocław and Tricity are now essentially equivalent for mid/senior B2B contractors.
04 · Remote
Remote-first Western = most lucrative path
Remote contracts with Western clients remain the single most lucrative option for Polish seniors.
Pay growth leaders
AI/ML · +15%
Top category for B2B rate growth. Data overtook JavaScript as the largest category at 10.78% of offers.
Process leverage
+40%
Acceptance-rate uplift for 2–3 week processes with clear communication (MOTIFE Kraków Market Map 2025).
Sub-section 5.2

Data caveats · what stops you over-indexing

Different methodologies tell different stories. These five caveats stop any single report from misleading you.

  1. Methodologies differ by 15–20%. Offer data (NFJ, JJIT) skews high · community self-reporting (Bulldogjob) skews low. The gap is real, not noise.
  2. No authoritative UoP-vs-B2B share figure exists for software engineers specifically — only directional survey data.
  3. 2026 fiscal parameters assume post-veto law. The Presidential veto reversed the 2025 health-contribution reform. Further legislative change is possible.
  4. Junior salary ranges are noisy. Offer volume is small (4.79% of JJIT 2025) and compositional effects dominate the medians.
  5. Sub-top-4 city data is weakly sampled. Poznań, Łódź, Lublin, Katowice breakdowns should be used only as rough guides.
Sub-section 5.3

Final action list · run this week

If you're actively planning a move or a negotiation, this is the four-line plan.

Action 01
Benchmark on Just Join IT & NoFluffJobs

Mandatory salary ranges are your negotiation anchor. The published upper bound is a ceiling, not an expectation — real hires land 17–19% below it.

Action 02
Structure compensation as B2B ryczałt 12% above ~18k PLN

The crossover point is stable across Polish tax calculators. Below 12k UoP wins on benefits; above 18k B2B wins on net cash.

Action 03
Target mid-to-senior roles · Data, AI/ML, cloud or Java/Python backend

These are the categories paying the most in 2025–2026 and the only ones that escaped the senior-rate decline.

Action 04
Optimise for process speed & transparent feedback

Worth a 40% uplift in acceptance rates on its own, according to MOTIFE's Kraków market map. Silent rejection remains the #1 candidate complaint.
