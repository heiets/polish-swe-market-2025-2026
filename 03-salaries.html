Chapter 03 · Salaries

Backend & frontend bands, junior to staff.

Three data sources disagree on absolute levels — offer data (NFJ, JJIT) runs 15–20% above self-reported — but agree on structure. B2B net-on-invoice runs 60–80% higher than UoP gross. Seniors earn 2.5–3.5× juniors.

Backend pays 5–10% more than frontend
Architecture is the highest-paid category by a distance at 28,370 PLN net B2B median for seniors. Data overtook JavaScript as the single largest category at 10.78% of offers, and AI/ML recorded +15% on B2B contracts — the top category for pay growth.
Sub-section 3.4

City & trend data

Warsaw remains top for absolute compensation, but Kraków has closed the gap. Compositional effect: Warsaw's median is dragged down by junior corporate/SSC staff, while Kraków skews toward product engineering and R&D.
