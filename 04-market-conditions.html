Chapter 04 · Market & hiring

Pulse, perks & process · the shape of the 2026 hire.

Demand shifted decisively from "bodies" to "impact." Remote/hybrid is definitive. Benefits are trending toward time, not perks. Interviews are shorter, but the 40% acceptance-rate uplift still belongs to the employers who move fast and respond clearly.

Just Join IT · 2026 report commentary
Return-to-Office pressure is a retention threat
86% of IT specialists would consider changing jobs if forced into more office presence (Next Technology Professionals 2025). Poland has been a net beneficiary of nearshoring as AI-driven layoffs at Meta, Google, Microsoft and Intel redistribute work toward stable CEE locations.

Sub-section 4.3

English, work permits & benefits

Language, legal status and perks in one slab — the three levers foreign candidates and employers most often miss.

Language
English B2/C1 required

Vast majority of mid and senior offers in R&D centres, product companies and international consultancies.

  • Polish rarely a hard requirement for engineering roles.
  • Preferred for manager-track positions in Polish-owned enterprises, banking and public-sector-adjacent work.
Non-EU permits
EU Blue Card · threshold 12,272.60 PLN gross

Dominant path for IT specialists. 150% of the national average, up ~15% from 2024.

  • May 2025 amendment cut minimum contract to 6 months.
  • Employer change without card reissuance after 2 years.
  • Eligibility expanded for 3 years' relevant experience in the last 7 (no degree needed).
  • Alternative: zezwolenie na pobyt czasowy i pracę — lower threshold, no EU mobility, slower (months to a year per voivodeship).
Typical benefits package
The near-universal seven
  • Private healthcare — Medicover, Luxmed, Enel-Med.
  • Multisport / MedicoverSport / Benefit Systems card.
  • Training budget 2,000–5,000 PLN/yr + paid conferences.
  • Laptop + monitor + peripherals.
  • Home-office one-off stipend 1,000–3,000 PLN.
  • Paid leave above statutory is rare; some offer "wellbeing / wiedza" days.
  • Equity rare outside foreign R&D (Google, Meta, Nvidia, ARM, Motorola) and PL scale-ups (Allegro, DocPlanner, Brainly, Booksy). Bonuses 5–15% more typical.
Cultural shift toward time, not perks
Bulldogjob 2023 found the top-desired benefits in 2023 became a 4-day work week and flexible hours — not private healthcare or training. The preference has persisted into 2025–2026 and reshapes retention strategy more than any cash bonus.
Sub-section 4.4

Interview funnel & negotiation norms

Typical Polish IT funnel runs 3–4 stages over 2–4 weeks. Multi-day take-homes are out; live pair-programming is in.

1
Recruiter / HR screening
30–45 min · motivation, expectations, visa status
2
Technical interview
1–2 engineers · stack & reasoning
3
Practical task
Live pair-programming or whiteboard — pushback against unpaid take-homes
4
Manager / behavioural
Sometimes combined with informal team meet · system-design rounds at mid/senior in product cos.
Negotiation room
5–15%
Typical uplift above first offer · mid / senior

Levers: signing bonus, additional leave on UoP, increased training budget, higher B2B day rate tied to a longer commitment.

Counter-offers from current employer are common and often match — but retention after a counter is poor.

The published max is a ceiling
17–19%
Actual B2B hires pay below the upper advertised range

Just Join IT "Eldorado" analysis — published maximum is a negotiation anchor, not an expected number.
